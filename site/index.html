    Agents that speak the Program Status Protocol (OSC 7501) — Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, pi, Crush and more — are believed word for word. Every other agent is read from its window title, progress and output. Nothing is installed to do either.
    
  
    

    


    
  
    Watch one full turn.
    
  
    The shortcut opens the picker. An agent launches into its own editor tab with its own icon. When it hits a permission prompt, a notification fires, and Show jumps straight back to the tab that asked.
    
  

    


    
  
    Small, deliberately.
    
  
    
    
    
      
    Your order, not ours
    
      
    Launches are ranked by frecency — the agents you actually use float to the top, and the order syncs across machines.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Rename without leaving
    
      
    Press R in the picker and rename the tab you are about to jump to. Tracking follows the new name.
